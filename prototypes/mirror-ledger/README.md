# Mirror Ledger: standalone experiment

Two 4×4 boards use the existing Sun & Moon balance, no-triples and distinct-line rules. Numbered same/opposite clues connect cells across the two boards. Both boards are visible together on a wide screen and stack on small screens; a link button outlines the endpoints, and link numbers and screen-reader labels identify them without colour. It is an independent memory-only prototype: serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server 8798` and visit `/prototypes/mirror-ledger/`. Undo keeps the last 40 changed states. Reload resets progress. There is no telemetry, rating, persistence, app registration, or offline installation.

## Design choices and limits

A single board with adjacent links already exists as Eclipse Lab's Constellation. Two independent boards would make comparison optional. Cross-board links were chosen so each authored pair requires the relationship for uniqueness; all individual boards have multiple solutions on their own. Another possible design is a movable mask, but its placement, undo and equivalent-mask semantics need a separate experiment. These are design alternatives, not claims of discovery: [MatMod's Binairo formulation](https://matmod.ch/lpl/PDF/binairo.pdf) documents the underlying balanced, distinct binary lines and no-triples rules, while [LinkedIn's Tango rules](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6861672/) describe same/opposite links on one grid. Neither establishes novelty of this combination.

The eight deterministic fixtures are provisional, not ranked by human difficulty. A finite solver enumerates the entire board-pair space. Hints report only values common to every completion consistent with current entries and both boards; they do not consult an answer. Contradictory entries yield no hint. `node --test tests/mirror-ledger.test.mjs` independently enumerates all 65,536 binary boards, checks pair uniqueness, ambiguity of each separate board, and verifies that removing all cross-board links restores ambiguity. Every authored link is also necessary for uniqueness, checked separately. Automated evidence cannot establish fairness, accessibility on physical devices, enjoyment, learning or originality.

Ask consenting players to explain an inference across a link before using Hint, and observe whether stacked boards and endpoint labels remain usable on a phone. Record confusion, backtracking and whether links add reasoning rather than bookkeeping. Do not treat a completed solve as evidence of understanding or relative difficulty. A later app integration would require its own storage, backup, offline, budget and actual-control checks.
