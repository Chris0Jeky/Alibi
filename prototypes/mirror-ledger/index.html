Mirror Ledger

Two ledgers share clues. Use what you learn on one board to complete the other.

How to play

Fill both boards with suns and moons. Each row and column has two of each; three identical symbols cannot be consecutive. Completed rows must differ from one another, as must columns. Each numbered link joins one cell on the left ledger to one on the right: same means matching symbols; opposite means different symbols.

Select a cell to cycle empty → sun → moon. With a keyboard, use arrow keys within a board, S for sun, M for moon, Backspace to clear, and Enter or Space to cycle.
