# Alibi asset direction

Baseline: `897651e53a27b29e2b9a0a55d75b474ee0de88dc`, version 0.6.0, build
`f7c4ef0b6961`. Sources: actual local Club, collection, casebook and Quiet Wing screens;
seven original gouache covers; current companion illustrations and modular city geometry.

The club is a place to notice things. Petrol `#173e49`, ink `#172d38`, warm paper
`#f4ead4`, lamplight `#dbac60`, sage `#87a997`, terracotta `#b76d52`. Keep existing
theme tokens for controls. Cream silhouettes against petrol carry small illustrations;
amber is light, not a universal success signal. Errors retain text and symbols.

Mystery illustrations: editorial cut-paper silhouettes, restrained stipple and curved
coastal contours, upper-left light, no characters implicated by composition. Existing
gouache remains the large-cover medium. New exact SVG vignettes are deliberately graphic,
not represented as new painterly originals. Keep central subjects within the middle 70%
for mobile crops. No lettering, grids, numbers, culprit hints or solved evidence in art.

Badges: 100-unit master, recognisable 24-unit central symbol, consistent medallion and
ribbon, separate locked neutral state. Different silhouettes, not recolours, identify
awards. Text names and existing unlock criteria remain adjacent. No invented achievements.
Precise category icons and instructional boards reuse the existing programmatic system;
teaching examples remain interactive and are checked by the existing rule/control tests.

Companions: reuse current species shapes and colours; gentle attention, rounded contours,
stable ground anchors. Realm: matte materials, consistent grid and pivots, geometry that
survives rotation and export. Their preview states are not additional game mechanics.

Typography stays editable: existing serif headlines and system sans-serif controls.
Art never replaces control labels. Use at least 44px touch targets and visible keyboard
focus. Zen hides decoration; no new art is inserted into a puzzle board.

Motion: slow settles, brief anticipation, no incessant celebration. Every interactive
preview offers pause/static presentation and honours reduced motion. Audio is soft wood,
paper, water and lamplight; explicit playback only, mute preserved, no autoplay soundtrack.

Pilot review: inspect the first vignette, category card/icon, earned/locked stamp at 24/48/96px,
companion action, realm scene, cue and HyperFrames start/middle/end frames before scaling.
Ledger records actual evidence separately from pending auditory/physical acceptance.

Rights: retain the repository's current source-rights decision. New local designs are
original project work with no separate public reuse grant. Reused third-party materials
retain their existing asset-specific licences and attribution. No paid submissions.
