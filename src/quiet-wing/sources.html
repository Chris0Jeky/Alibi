Art beside the games

The Club, puzzle collection and garden also feature three Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access images, each explicitly identified as public domain in its retained API record:

  • Camille Corot, A Woman Reading, 1869 and 1870.
  • George Cochran Lambdin, Side of a Greenhouse, 1870–80 (?).
  • William Trost Richards, Near Land’s End, Cornwall, 1879.

These are credited museum works, not Alibi characters or case evidence. Images are resized and encoded locally without generative alteration. Original-resolution files, complete records, credit lines and checksums are retained in assets-source/atmosphere.
