# Casebook follow-up

## The unfinished invitation is shipped

The former editorial seed is now a published, continuous eight-record casebook. Its source is
[`content/extra/case-invitation.json`](../content/extra/case-invitation.json), its casebook
registration is [`content/casebooks.json`](../content/casebooks.json), and
[`tests/case-invitation.test.cjs`](../tests/case-invitation.test.cjs) covers its chronology and
published puzzle definitions. Those definitions, IDs and revisions remain the current saved-game
contract; this note does not propose changing them.

The shipped source establishes the Pike & Tide sequence: the copper plate, fresh stock, tide-mark,
drying-room access, sealed envelope, commission ledger, old seal, and final organizer account.
It distinguishes opportunity and access from authorship and responsibility in the player-facing
records. Further story changes belong in a separately scoped casebook change, with the same
definition and revision safeguards.

## Current casebook framing

Bellweather is a continuous six-record mystery and The unfinished invitation is a continuous
eight-record mystery. Briar House, Night Train and Glasshouse are anthologies of standalone
records. Their frames should describe that distinction without implying a causal continuation
that the published records do not provide.
