## Candidate: The unfinished invitation

A coastal print shop has sent invitations to a gathering that nobody admits arranging.
An apprentice finds one final envelope still sealed, addressed to someone who left town years ago.
This is an editorial seed, not committed canon or a shipped puzzle.

Desired rhythm: short opening → witness comparison → story page → spatial reconstruction →
story page → ledger deduction → final conclusion → epilogue. Each solved record must establish
one fact needed by the next scene; the final answer should be inferable before it is revealed.
Use new IDs, original clues and consistent people/objects/times, with preserved saved definitions.

Before production: choose the central mystery and ending, write a chronology and evidence table,
verify every puzzle has one semantic solution, test the actual controls, and ask a player to
follow the story without reveals. Do not substitute extra level count for a coherent case.
