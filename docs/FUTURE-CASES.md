## Candidate: The unfinished invitation

A coastal print shop has sent invitations to a gathering that nobody admits arranging.
An apprentice finds one final envelope still sealed, addressed to someone who left town years ago.
This is an editorial seed, not committed canon or a shipped puzzle.

Desired rhythm: short opening → witness comparison → story page → spatial reconstruction →
story page → ledger deduction → final conclusion → epilogue. Each solved record must establish
one fact needed by the next scene; the final answer should be inferable before it is revealed.
Use new IDs, original clues and consistent people/objects/times, with preserved saved definitions.

Before production: choose the central mystery and ending, write a chronology and evidence table,
verify every puzzle has one semantic solution, test the actual controls, and ask a player to
follow the story without reveals. Do not substitute extra level count for a coherent case.

## Evidence outline for editorial development

At Pike & Tide, new unsigned invitations carry the tide-mark design of Iona Reed, an apprentice
who left after a shop fire. Distinguish these fresh invitations from an old, unposted envelope
containing her claim to the original design. The proposed organizer is master printer Orrin Pike;
the player must establish who commissioned the new run separately from who created the design.

1. Witness comparison establishes who retrieved Iona's printing plate and when.
2. The continuation explains why its distinctive mark connects the fresh invitations to Iona.
3. Spatial reconstruction establishes who could access the drying room and hidden envelope.
   Access establishes opportunity, not motive or guilt.
4. The continuation identifies what the old letter could prove; the print run's organizer remains open.
5. Ledger deduction connects paper stock, ink batch, job number and courier receipt to the commission.
6. The player infers the organizer before the conclusion. The epilogue restores Iona's authorship.

Unresolved editorial question: why would the organizer stage a gathering to control the old evidence?
Settle that motive and the envelope's chronology before treating the outline as canon or writing puzzles.
No new puzzle definitions or IDs are created by this proposal.
