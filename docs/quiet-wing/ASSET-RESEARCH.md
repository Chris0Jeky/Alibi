# Expansion asset research — 2026-09-08

Read-only Luna investigation, reconciled with the downloaded pack. Research is not integration.

Kenney Castle Kit 2.0 contains 76 static models, with a local CC0 licence. GLB, OBJ and FBX are
available; GLB and OBJ reference a separate colour atlas. There are no animal or building
animations in that pack. Its models are suitable for modular towers, walls, arches, stairs,
bridges and trees. Preserve the colour atlas and original licence when processing models.

- Castle source: https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit
- Cohesive homes: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
- Additional roads: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
- Building modules: https://kenney.nl/assets/building-kit

- https://gobkit.itch.io/gobkit-free-animal-pack-vol-2 — CC0 Owl and other animals.

- Quaternius is not selected: its pack page and updated general licence conflict over standalone
  redistribution. Do not infer export rights from a thumbnail marked CC0.
