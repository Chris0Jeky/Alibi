Branch `claude/folio-3d-893`, cut from `origin/main` 9116194e. Items 1-3 and the receipt-focus LOW
item of #893. The other LOW items (generic Castle wrong-answer text, Art room rights line, empty
search grid, locked-room hash, Realm toast) are untouched.

## Changed

- `tools/build.cjs`: `connect-src` gains `blob:` (and only `connect-src`). GLTFLoader fetches the GLB's
  embedded textures from blob URLs, which Chromium counts as `connect-src`. The one `documentPolicy`
  string feeds both the `<meta>` CSP in `dist/index.html` and the `Content-Security-Policy` line in
  `dist/_headers`, so Cloudflare and the local server get the same value. The Sites fallback
  (`.openai/hosting.json`, retired 2026-09-27) publishes the same `dist`, so it has no separate
  header. The native Android bundle has its own `NATIVE_CSP` (`connect-src 'self'`) but ships no
  Field notes or three.js, so it is untouched. Emitted:
  `connect-src 'self' blob: https://images.pexels.com https://images.unsplash.com https://pulseboard-observatory.commit-atlas.workers.dev`.
- `src/castle/case-player.mjs`: a "Next hint" press once the stage's three hints are shown saves
  nothing, does not call `receipt.hint`, and sets "All hints are shown." instead of "Hint shown."
  The button stays enabled (a disabled button would drop keyboard focus when the last hint is
  taken); the status line is the signal. The now-redundant `Math.min(3, ...)` became `++`.
  "Start local playtest receipt" now passes the receipt's own focus mapping (`receipt.focus`)
  to `run()`, so focus lands on "Review receipt".
- `src/quiet-wing/folio.js`: the Turn/zoom buttons render with `disabled`, are disabled again when a
  piece is chosen, and are enabled only once the scene view's `ready` promise resolves with the
  `folio-3d` canvas mounted (not on a failed load or context loss at open).
- `tests/castle-assets.test.cjs`: the optional player's bound `< 16 KiB` becomes `< 16 KiB + 64`.
  The player went 16,362 -> 16,397 bytes (+35); excess 14 over 16,383; ceiling +64 leaves 50; no
  feature room.
- `tests/csp-blob-connect.test.cjs` (new): `dist/_headers` and the `index.html` meta both allow `blob:`
  in `connect-src` and nowhere in `default-src`, `script-src`, `style-src` or `object-src`.
- `tests/browser_experience.py`: Turn/zoom disabled before Open 3D and again after choosing another
  piece, enabled once 3D is open; opening a Field notes model ("Wall") produces no
  Content-Security-Policy / `Couldn't load texture` console message.
- `tests/browser_castle_case.py`: focus lands on Review receipt after Start; after the last hint,
  five more presses leave saved hints at 3, the receipt hint count at only the effective presses and
  the status at "All hints are shown.".
