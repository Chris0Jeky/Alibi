## Before solving
In the tester’s own words, what is the goal? Which rules are easy to miss? What does each symbol mean? Could the title or image spoil the answer?

## Solve record
Record meaningful deductions, not every click. Note the first stall, every guess/backtrack, any inaccurate or unhelpful hint, and accidental input. Keep board-state snapshots at confusion points. Do not infer a difficulty rating from total elapsed time alone.

## Verdict
Was there one clear satisfying idea? Did the finish feel earned? Was the result understandable without revealing hidden assumptions? Were undo, notes, selection and confirmation predictable? Is the artwork supportive rather than distracting?
