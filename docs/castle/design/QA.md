# Verification and limits

## Result

This standalone prototype passed **23 Node tests** and **120 actual-control browser checks**, with 60 checks each at 390px and 1440px widths. The final build includes the optional 18-second film. Per-width JSON reports, Node output, screenshots and build hashes are in `verification/`.

These are different kinds of evidence. Pure logic/state tests use synthetic adapters. Chromium controls run in an injected document because browser navigation to file and local-server origins was blocked by the authoring environment. The storage warning visible in screenshots is the truthful session-only result for that document, not a simulated claim of browser persistence.

## Logic and state checks

The Node suite enumerates or solves the authored boards where appropriate: a unique gate combination, unique shelf order, unique lamp-switch parity solution, all eight Lo Shu symmetries, the seven-move minimum Hanoi solution, the unique qualifying path under the stated travel times, the impossibility of traversing all seven bridges once, and the sixteen outcomes of the modern four-fair-binary-dice model.

It also exercises room reachability, alternate museum access, unique first-completion rewards, worked-solution provenance, cloned/reloaded state, bounded drafts and notes, malformed and future state protection, stale-writer refusal, quota failure, and raw-record recovery export. There is no random content generation masquerading as hand-authored puzzle validation.

## Browser controls

Each viewport run completes all ten activities through their actual controls. It checks incorrect-answer rejection, keyboard focus after input, selected swap state, route undo, the 21:00 / seven-minute deductions, rejection of an overconfident story conclusion, access to the secret stair, all three museum activities and their source links, 100 points without repeat farming, worked-reveal persistence across closing/reopening, escaped notes, evidence collection, room search and honest planned-room labels.

The suite also checks modal horizontal bounds, reduced-motion and quiet-story settings, Escape to close, no runtime exceptions, and the embedded film's size/duration, lack of autoplay and text alternative. Desktop and phone-sized estate, room, map-puzzle, museum and notebook captures were visually reviewed. A narrow-map overlap was corrected during the pass; numbered map controls were enlarged to usable touch targets. Unfocused skip-link clipping was hardened for scrolled full-page captures.

## Film and assets

The MP4 is 1280 x 720, H.264, 18 seconds, and silent. It is an original illustrated mood sequence, not a capture of production Alibi. Captions are part of the composition; a VTT and transcript are supplied. The full preview embeds the film for portability; the lite preview omits it. This packaging choice is not the recommended production loading strategy.

Room and estate geometry is original SVG. No new Blender pet models or commercial game assets were created or copied. No paid provider credits were used, and no font files are distributed.

## Not verified or not implemented

No full Alibi checkout was available in this environment, so the current repository was not built, tested, patched, merged or deployed. Selective connected reads informed the integration guide. Concurrent repository changes can supersede that snapshot.

Real-origin prototype storage, real browser restart/recovery, service-worker/offline behaviour, production cross-tab persistence, old/new-release upgrades, hosted headers, Android/TalkBack, sustained GPU performance, audio quality and external Blender/editor round-trips are not certified by these results. Synthetic state tests do not replace them.

The prototype provides scoped export, not a restore/import UI. Browser back/forward integration into native Alibi routes remains production work. Optional chimes and the film are not required to solve any activity. Reduced-motion and text alternatives are implemented but do not constitute a full accessibility conformance audit.

Only Chapter I is playable. Chapters II–V are authored narrative/quest plans whose final puzzle definitions still need independent checking and human playtests. The ten initial activities are deliberately compact demonstrations of the larger loop, not a replacement catalogue for Alibi's 324 puzzles. The 22 planned rooms remain visibly planned even at 100 points.

The companion, garden, building-kit and realm-expansion systems are deferred issues, not partially advertised runtime features. Full historical coverage, more complex difficulty calibration and sustained human engagement are also unproven.
