historical claims and mathematical examples, not evidence for fictional events at Wrenmere.
The supplied [museum editorial plan](design/MUSEUM-EDITORIAL.md) preserves the larger programme.

The curatorial loop is **handle → notice → learn → use**. Each table first exposes an
interactive question, then its source-backed history and a label-revision exercise. A wrong
label receives specific feedback and can be revised without penalty. Three reviewed labels
open a fictional exhibition draft showing Mara's concern with ordinary use and repair.
That drawer is original story writing, not a quoted historical artefact or a flood deduction.

The evidence board uses the same discipline: attach collected records, distinguish an open
question from a provisional assessment, and revise it when needed. Player assessments never
change the chapter's evidence or unlocks. Eight bounded hypotheses and three curatorial
records are included in castle exports, imports and pre-restore recovery. Original version-1
notebooks acquire empty defaults; older readers protect unfamiliar fields rather than erasing them.
