# The unfinished invitation

This continuous casebook follows one morning at Pike & Tide, a coastal print shop. It adds eight
new records without changing any published puzzle definition or saved revision. The central mystery
is authorship and control of evidence: Orrin Pike wants a harbour commission to treat Iona Reed's
tide-mark as shop property before the audit, so he stages a gathering that can manufacture witnesses
and collect old samples.

The floor-plan record is deliberately limited. It identifies who had an opportunity to reach Celia
Wren's packet; it establishes opportunity only and cannot establish motive, authorship or guilt. The later ledger and final account
are required before the organizer is named.

## Editorial resolution

The 2022 fire interrupted Iona's signed claim before it could be posted. On 9 September 2026,
Orrin used her plate for a fresh harbour commission and staged a gathering to make former staff
repeat his version of the shop's history and surrender old samples before the audit. Elias moved the
sealed envelope because he was the courier, not because the floor plan makes him responsible. The
commission ledger and final witness count identify Orrin as organizer; the dated seal identifies Iona
as the original designer.
