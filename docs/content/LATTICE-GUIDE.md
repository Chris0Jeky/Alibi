# Lattice editorial openings

Optional spoiler-aware guide for 24 original Workshop studies. Coordinates are A–G / 1–7. These are visible-clue constraints, not complete walkthroughs. Every Expert label is provisional.

For Lanterns, a numbered wall fixes an exact count among its adjacent floor squares. A square’s listed light sources are the visible floor positions remaining after zero-clue exclusions. Other clues may eliminate more sources. Compare these two sets before committing a lantern.

For Futoshiki, a strictly increasing chain of k cells gives its first cell an upper bound of 8−k and its last a lower bound of k. The chains below turn between axes. Bounds are not assignments; combine them with the six givens and row/column exclusions.

The elementary-profile remainder is an instrumented stopping point, not human difficulty or proof that guessing is necessary. No complete no-guess or human-playtest claim is made.
